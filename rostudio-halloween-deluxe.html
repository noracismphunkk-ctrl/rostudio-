THE BEGINNING · OCTOBER 2026

One month ago, RoStudio began.

RoStudio was created by Mimic about one month ago, inspired by CoStudio. The idea was to take inspiration from CoStudio and build a project with its own name, identity, and direction. Since then, RoStudio has been shaped into a creator-focused experience for Studio Lite. This is only the beginning, with more ideas and features still ahead.
